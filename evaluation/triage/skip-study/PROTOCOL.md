# Skip-aware measurement correction

This is a diagnostic rerun of the same two application tasks, with fresh reviewers;
it is not a new held-out application sample. Prior outcomes informed the method
correction. The earlier source, gold, prompts and logs remain frozen. Do not pool
cohorts or attribute a before/after difference solely to the new CLI option.

Question: does adopted Serene let reviewers omit individual construction screening
of recognized sites, while retaining dangerous-path discovery with less delivered
review context? Compare conventional Raw source and the same uniform Serene conversion.
No Rules/layout, semantic authorization or business correctness scoring.

Four fresh Luna/medium sessions, one per task/arm, at most two concurrent. Use order
s01 billing Raw, s02 billing Serene, s03 warehouse Serene, s04 warehouse Raw. No retries,
no model substitutions. Source tasks and gold are inherited unchanged from efficiency-study.
The new controller, CLI, participant prompt, source/gold hashes and numeric caps must
be frozen before dispatch. Served snapshot/token usage are unavailable.

Both arms have file listing, normal regex/literal search, range reads and targeted
function reads with import context. `investigate` returns only the owning function
and directly indexed helper/caller spans, never an entire unrelated store file.
Non-sink helpers can also be read by function name. These targeted facilities are
identical in both arms. Serene also has compact audit and support explaining its
construction-only skip contract. No instruction forces all-site deep investigation.

Budget: 32,768 bytes of actual serialized client response text (including framing,
errors and newline), 80 requests, 600 seconds, 23 unique context-expanded sites as a
nonbinding safety ceiling. Fixed-byte checkpoints 4,096 / 8,192 / 16,384 / 32,768 are
observations of this single trajectory, not separately randomized smaller-budget runs.
Control/error responses can overshoot the byte cap; all are charged. No additional
source payload is delivered after its cap. Time includes scheduling. Bytes are not
called tokenizer counts or monetary cost. Prompt length is separately archived.

Primary reporting units per task/arm:
- Correct dangerous-path discovery at the actual byte budget and at checkpoints.
- Actual response bytes and successful read/search/function/investigate/audit actions
  up to the first correctly submitted diagnosis and to finish; preserve no-hit censoring.
- Unique execution-site owner bodies exposed by source tools, including initial
  screening. A match to any body line counts exposure; this is deliberately conservative
  about incidental search hits, not an assertion of mental work or proof of reading.
- Audit-ordinary execution count eligible for skipping; among these, owner bodies
  never exposed before diagnosis/finish. Call the latter observed source omission,
  not proven cognitive skip. Audit summary is not counted as source-body exposure.
- Ordinary bodies that were exposed, source-line/response-volume overhead, and N
  helper/caller exposure. Do not treat screening of 20 Raw sites as zero work just
  because it does not expand imported context. Do not penalize justified N resolution.

Gold independent of audit still determines D and N. Count false findings and false
safe dismissals separately. Audit may have unknown coverage; summary counts are never
proof of whole-program completeness or semantic safety. No assumed 20-site saving:
compare actual ordinary-body exposure/omission in both arms and charged output.

This small diagnostic cohort has no significance threshold or general product verdict.
Evidence of skip use requires preserved D detection together with ordinary bodies
omitted and a measured cost reduction, reported separately rather than a composite
score. If the agent elects to reread ordinary source, retain that cost and report the
behavior; never subtract it after the fact. Neither gold nor experimental source may
be changed after answers. Independent finding adjudication uses shuffled case IDs
with no cost metadata; source representation may reveal arm. Failures are retained.

Calibration uses the old repetitive synthetic sources only, with a fresh Luna/medium
participant to verify compact delivery, targeted function access and charged output.
Local tests verify serialization counts and refusal at caps. This does not make shared
filesystem mediation physically isolated. Freeze and execute without another human
planning gate as already authorized. Keep PR Draft.
