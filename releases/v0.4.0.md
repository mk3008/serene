# Serene 0.4.0

Serene 0.4.0 adds a second, independent review axis for recognized fixed SQL: lightweight content triage that asks for a closer look without claiming SQL correctness or safety.

### SQL-content review

- Recognized `sql` tags can carry `reviewSignals` while their construction level remains `ordinary`.
- The audit suggests review for destructive or operationally exceptional patterns such as `DROP`, `TRUNCATE`, `RENAME`, temporary-table creation, and `SELECT` / `UPDATE` / `DELETE` without an apparent `WHERE`.
- Data-modifying CTE candidates are surfaced when a CTE body begins with `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, or `DELETE`; unsupported or unclear CTE body starts are referred with `SQL_UNRESOLVED_CTE` instead of requiring a full SQL parser.
- Heuristics intentionally prefer review over certainty. False positives are acceptable, and absence of a signal is not approval of SQL meaning, authorization, performance, or binding correctness.

### Review delivery

- Content suggestions stay visible in `--actionable-only` even when construction is otherwise ordinary.
- Source and diff filters do not compress away SQL carrying a content review signal.
- Content suggestions remain advisory and do not affect normal or `--strict` exit status; existing construction violation/review-required behavior is unchanged.

### Scope

- Inspection remains limited to SQL already recognized through Serene's `sql` tag.
- Arbitrary strings, `.sql` / migration files, ORM/query-builder code, and general SQL parsing remain outside Serene's scope.
